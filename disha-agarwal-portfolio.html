01 01 / Profile

About me

I'm a Cyber Security undergraduate who got curious about the other side of the screen — how a secure system can also feel simple and pleasant to use. That curiosity pulled me toward Figma, Canva and front-end basics alongside penetration-testing concepts and threat modelling.

My projects usually sit at that intersection: a phishing-detection tool that needed a clear alerting flow, a civic-reporting concept that needed transparent status tracking, an emergency device concept that needed to work under pressure with almost no interface at all.

I'm currently exploring the Innovation and Management Programme at Bern University of Applied Sciences, Switzerland (2026), and looking for opportunities where security thinking and design thinking can work in the same room.
